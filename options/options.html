Migalhas

Migalhas needs access to all sites to list cookies and to protect open tabs. Turn it on in the extension's details (Site access: On all sites). Until then, the clean-up is paused.

Automatic clean-up

Sites with cookies

On: cookies are kept. Off: cookies are deleted at the next clean-up, unless the site is open in a tab. New sites start switched off.

Lock a site with its padlock so the switch can't change by accident.

The buttons apply to the sites shown, so you can search first. Locked sites are skipped.
